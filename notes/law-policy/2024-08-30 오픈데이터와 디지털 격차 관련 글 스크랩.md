https://towardsdatascience.com/is-there-a-difference-between-open-data-and-public-data-6261cd7b5389  

Source: "Bridging the Digital Divide" (ThinkData Works blog) https://blog.thinkdataworks.com/bridging-the-digital-divide  
Summary: Open/public data is published but remains hard to use (inconsistent standards, PDFs, messy feeds), leaving a gap between data holders and data users.  
Neither governments nor end users should carry the burden of normalizing it; the article argues for intermediary tools and processes that make data usable at scale.
